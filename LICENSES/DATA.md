This grant does not confer rights the project does not hold. TAFRA/openAFRICA resources marked CC BY 4.0 retain that license; parliamentary resources, HCP data, and other sources retain the terms declared by their producers. Content with unconfirmed reuse terms must not be redistributed merely because it was processed by this project. Reuse must preserve source identifiers and the attributions in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
